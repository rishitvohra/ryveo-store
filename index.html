THE QUICK DETAILS

GOOD TO KNOW.

Everything you need before making your RYVEO pick.

How do I place an order?

Choose your design, select the colour and size, add it to your cart and continue to WhatsApp checkout. RYVEO will confirm your order details there.

Can I pay online on the website?

Online payment is not enabled. Payment details are confirmed directly with RYVEO on WhatsApp when your order is discussed.

How much is delivery?

Orders with a discounted subtotal of ₹1,000 or more unlock free delivery. For other orders, the delivery charge is shown in your cart and reconfirmed by RYVEO.

Can I request a custom print?

Yes. Share your idea and design through the Custom Print Studio. RYVEO will confirm feasibility, final pricing and timeline before accepting the request.

How do I choose my size?

Check the size options on each product page. If you are unsure about the oversized fit or garment measurements, message RYVEO before ordering.
